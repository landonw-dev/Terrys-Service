ABOUT THE BUSINESS

ROOTED IN EXPERIENCE.

Established in 1987, Terry's Auto Sales and Salvage has been serving the automotive needs of customers for 39 years. Located in Waverly, Kansas, the business specializes in automotive repair, helping customers get their vehicles back on the road.

In addition to mechanic work, Terry's offers used auto parts and salvage options for customers looking for replacement parts. With a long-standing history and an A+ rating from the Better Business Bureau, Terry's is proud of its place in the local community.
